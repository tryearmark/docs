<Update label="August 7, 2026">
## Earmark can recover from a stuck session

Earmark can now detect the rare case where a long-lived desktop or browser session stops loading authenticated data and refresh once to recover. Checks run after events such as waking a laptop, returning after a long time in the background, or reconnecting to the internet. Recovery waits until capture is fully finished, so it does not interrupt an active meeting.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed **Mute microphone** on desktop muting system audio too. Other participants can now keep flowing into transcription while your microphone is muted.
- Fixed Stop and Resume disagreeing about microphone mute state, which could leave a resumed meeting silently muted even though the control looked unmuted.
- Fixed health recovery treating time spent asleep as a failed connection. Waking from a long suspension now starts a fresh check instead of immediately reloading or showing a recovery message.

</Update>

<Update label="July 31, 2026">
## Agentic Search has more context

Early-access Agentic Search can now look ahead to live Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook events for the next 14 days and combine them with authorized meeting history for preparation questions. Meetings can also be added to projects before, during, or after capture, so project context stays useful across the full meeting lifecycle.

## Share and reuse answers

Use **Copy as screenshot** on a completed response to capture the question, visible research steps, citations, and answer as a shareable image. Regular copy now preserves lists, links, emphasis, and tables when you paste into document editors. Answers with tables also offer separate **Copy for documents** and **Copy for chat** formats.

## More precise answers and sources

- Agentic Search now shows meeting citations as source chips instead of raw markers or internal IDs.
- Speaker-aware retrieval uses the right person's authorized recording when a project contains more than one capture of the same meeting, making questions such as “What did I say?” more reliable.
- Broad searches can continue through more meeting history and finish with a clearer statement of how much material was reviewed.
- Sending a chat message now shows an immediate **Thinking** state. You can keep drafting your next message while the answer runs, and a failed message keeps the newer draft intact while offering **Retry**.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed some paginated meeting searches failing when a relative date range changed by a few milliseconds between pages.
- Fixed copied Agentic Search answers including citation labels or internal source identifiers.

</Update>

<Update label="July 24, 2026">
## Agentic Search enters early access

Earmark's new Agentic Search experience is now in production for a limited rollout. It can answer broader questions across your authorized meetings, show the retrieval work behind an answer, and link evidence back to its source.

Projects add a shared place to group meetings, keep useful context, and ask questions within a focused body of work. The existing Earmark experience remains unchanged for workspaces that are not part of the early-access rollout.

</Update>

<Update label="June 26, 2026">
## Desktop notifications no longer get stuck

Desktop meeting notifications now clean up their native windows more defensively. Dismissing a notification, letting it time out, or replacing it with a newer one should no longer leave an invisible surface over the upper-right corner of the screen that intercepts clicks.

## Improvements

- Maximized source-attributed transcripts now show each turn in a distinct message bubble while preserving the existing **Me** and **Them** colors.
- Capture attempts now record a clearer diagnostic summary, helping support distinguish device, audio-signal, system-audio, network, and transcription failures without retaining raw audio.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed a runtime conflict that could break scrollable areas or popovers after interface component updates.

</Update>

<Update label="June 19, 2026">
## Source-attributed transcripts are live on desktop

Desktop capture now keeps microphone and system audio as separate transcription sources, so the transcript can distinguish what you said from what other meeting participants said with **Me** and **Them** labels. Search and AI retrieval use the same attribution, making questions such as “What did I say?” more reliable. Web and mobile capture continue to use a mixed transcript. See [Live transcription](/earmark-basics/transcription).

## Capture reliability improvements

- Desktop notification and toolbar starts now reconcile through the main app more reliably instead of depending on a single handoff arriving at exactly the right moment.
- Microphone transcription stays required while system-audio transcription can recover independently, so an optional system-audio problem is less likely to stop the entire capture.
- Transcript flushing now preserves late-finalized and overlapping turns more reliably.

</Update>

<Update label="June 12, 2026">
## Add sound to meeting notifications

The desktop app can now play a sound with the reminder shown one minute before a calendar meeting. Choose a sound in **Settings > Calendar**, hear a preview as you select it, or use **Show example** to test the full notification. The default remains **None**, so Earmark stays silent until you choose a sound. See [Connect your calendar](/getting-started/calendar).

## Meeting capture starts more reliably

Starting capture from a desktop notification or toolbar item now has a stateful handoff that can recover when the primary window is hidden, suspended, or slow to respond. The transcription path also detects startup failures and connected-but-silent stalls, then makes bounded recovery attempts instead of leaving capture in an ambiguous state.

</Update>

<Update label="June 5, 2026">
## Pin the moments that matter

You can now mark an important moment while a meeting is live, then use that moment as priority context for a focused artifact. Drop a pin from the meeting command bar, a global desktop shortcut, or the new floating Live Widget. See [Pins](/earmark-basics/pins) and [Live Widget](/earmark-basics/live-widget).

## Browse your full meeting history

The **Meetings** page now keeps loading older meetings as you scroll instead of stopping at a finite history limit. A date filter lets you jump to a specific day, and the calendar marks dates that contain meetings. See [Past meetings](/earmark-basics/meeting-history).

## Bug fixes

- Fixed a rare workspace membership sync issue that could block transcript loading, title generation, or default summaries even though the signed-in user still belonged to the workspace.

</Update>

<Update label="May 29, 2026">
## Platform upgrades with no workflow changes

Earmark moved its web application to Next.js 16 and its AI features to AI SDK 6. Existing generation, streaming, and artifact workflows keep the same behavior while the underlying platform stays current.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed a rare identity-cache mismatch that could leave a valid workspace member unable to load transcripts, generate meeting titles, or create the default meeting summary. Earmark now detects the missing membership state and repairs it from the signed-in session.

</Update>

Upcoming calendar meetings have **Rename meeting** and, when the calendar provider supplies the event link, **Open in calendar** for edits or deletion at the source.

## Mobile fullscreen composer

Tapping fullscreen on a task on mobile or smaller screen widths no longer hides the composer. The composer now lives in a left-side sheet that you can pull over the artifact to edit your prompt without leaving fullscreen. Drafts and update-as-you-type edits survive opening and closing the sheet, so a quick review does not cost you the prompt you were writing.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed an intermittent issue where dismissing a desktop meeting notification could leave a transparent native window alive in the upper-right of the screen, intercepting clicks meant for other apps. Restarting Earmark used to be the only way to clear it; now the close path starts a one-second native teardown watchdog and immediately stops the window from receiving mouse events.
- Fixed duplicate desktop update prompts. Shell updates and web client refreshes now share a single in-app `Update` badge in the sidebar, so you no longer see two competing prompts. If you are recording or in temporary mode, the action confirms before refreshing or restarting.

</Update>

## Calendar reconnect now reflects immediately in Settings

Connecting or reconnecting a Google or Microsoft calendar from **Settings** now updates the UI right away on desktop. Previously, the connection would succeed in the background but Settings would stay in its pre-connect state until you closed and reopened the panel — and even then the calendar toggles could show the wrong provider's calendars. That mismatch is fixed. See [Connect your calendar](/getting-started/calendar).

## Bug fixes

- Fixed Settings staying in a stale pre-connect state after returning from the desktop OAuth browser so the new connection is reflected without needing to close and reopen the panel.
- Fixed calendar toggles sometimes showing the wrong provider's calendars after a reconnect by isolating Google and Microsoft calendar lists in separate cache entries.
- Fixed the standalone connect dialog and the Settings calendar section so both react consistently when the app regains focus after the OAuth handoff.

</Update>
