New artifacts are now written by GPT-6 Sol, a month after the move to GPT-5.6 Terra. Expect artifacts that stay concise and to the point while being noticeably smarter about what matters. <Update label="September 18, 2026" description="Auto-end arrives">
## Recordings now end when your meeting does

Forgot to hit stop? The desktop app now ends a recording on its own once the meeting is over, so it doesn't keep capturing the next meeting or the hallway chat after it. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and many others.

Earmark follows your calendar to know when a meeting should wrap up, and it pays attention to the conversation too, so a meeting that runs long keeps recording until you're actually done. A recording also stops after 15 minutes without new speech, or when your computer goes to sleep.

Auto-end works on Mac and Windows with desktop app 1.8.0 or later, and needs macOS 14.2 or later on a Mac.

## The Live Widget is now opt-in

The floating Live Widget now starts turned off. If you like having pins one click away during meetings, turn it on in **Settings > Capture**. If you already chose on or off yourself, your choice stays as it was. If you never picked, the widget now starts off. See [Live Widget](/earmark-basics/live-widget).

<Showcase
  src="/images/changelog/2026-06-05/live-widget.png"
  alt="Capture settings showing the Live Widget toggle and a preview of the floating widget."
/>

## Bug fixes

- Fixed clicking **Pin** or dragging the Live Widget on the newest version of macOS bringing the main Earmark window forward and hiding the widget. The main window now stays in the background.

</Update>

<Update label="August 21, 2026" description="The big one">
## Meet Chat: ask anything about your meetings

Chat is now available to everyone. Ask a question and Earmark searches your meeting history, shows its research steps as it works, and answers with source chips that link back to the meetings it drew from.

<Showcase
  src="/images/chat/answer-with-sources.png"
  alt="A Chat answer listing Chotchkie's rollout requirements, with source chips after each point and a Coverage line."
/>

A few things to try:

- **"What did we decide about pricing last month?"** Chat searches across all your meetings, not just the one you're in.
- **"Help me prep for tomorrow's meetings."** Chat can look ahead two weeks in your Google or Outlook calendar and pair upcoming events with what was said before.
- **"What did I say about the launch date?"** Desktop recordings keep track of who said what, so first-person questions find your words, not someone else's.
- **Open Chat inside a meeting** to ask about just that conversation, live or after it ends, or to have Chat create an artifact for you.

## Organize work with Projects

Projects group related meetings, like a customer, a launch, or a hiring loop, so their context lives in one place. Add a meeting to a project before, during, or after it happens, share the project with teammates, and start a chat that stays focused on just that project.

To sort your history quickly, hover over meetings on the **Meetings** page to select them (Shift-click selects a range), then add the whole batch to a project at once.

<Showcase
  src="/images/projects/add-to-project.png"
  alt="The Meetings page with three meetings selected and the Add to project menu open."
/>

## Share answers anywhere

- **Copy as image** turns a response, including your question, the research steps, and the sources, into an image you can drop into Slack or a slide.
- Regular copy now keeps lists, links, and formatting intact. Answers with tables offer **Copy for documents** for Docs and Word, and **Copy for chat** for tools like Slack.

## Your week in Earmark

Once you've been using Earmark for a week, the home page offers a recap of your past seven days: work patterns, favorite phrases, your communication style, and a light roast. Don't worry, it's gentle. Dismiss it anytime.

## Improvements

- New **Show events with no attendees** setting in **Settings > Calendar**. Turn it off to hide placeholder events that no one else is invited to from Coming up, the toolbar, notifications, and Chat. It's on by default, so nothing changes unless you opt out, and meetings you've recorded always stay visible.
- The macOS menu bar countdown is now on by default. Turn it off with **Show countdown** in **Settings > Calendar**.
- Copy your own questions in Chat with one click.
- Workspace admins can check single sign-on status in **Settings > Workspace**, and contact us from there to set it up.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed desktop sign-in sometimes showing the "Signing in" progress screen twice.
- Fixed the Live Widget showing a full sign-in screen after you signed out. Signing out now closes it.

</Update>

<Update label="August 7, 2026" description="Self-healing app">
## Earmark unsticks itself

If Earmark loses its connection after your computer wakes up, reconnects to the internet, or sits in the background for a while, it now notices and reloads itself once, so you're not left staring at a loading sidebar. It waits for any recording to finish saving first. If a reload doesn't fix things, it asks you to refresh instead of trying again, and waking from a long sleep counts as a fresh start, not a failure.

- Fixed Stop and Resume disagreeing about mute state, which could leave a resumed meeting silently muted even though the button looked unmuted.
