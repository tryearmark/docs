## What it does

Connect your Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook to Earmark so your scheduled meetings appear automatically in the sidebar. Your day at a glance. Instantly see what's coming up, how many meetings you have left, and how long you have. Join and start capturing with one click.

Click a meeting to open it in Earmark. Long titles scroll into view when you hover.

Meetings that have passed their end time are automatically hidden unless they're actively being recorded.

### Declined and canceled meetings

Meetings you've declined and canceled events don't appear as upcoming meetings or trigger new reminders. Earmark uses your response to the invitation: if you organize a meeting and a guest declines, your meeting stays eligible for a reminder.

An already-open reminder closes when your calendar sends an explicit decline or cancellation. If declining removes the event from your calendar's feed, an open reminder can remain until you dismiss it.

### Hide events with no attendees

Placeholder events, like focus time or reminders you put on your own calendar, can crowd **Coming up**. Turn off **Show events with no attendees** in **Settings > Calendar** to hide events that no one else is invited to. They're also hidden from the toolbar, notifications, and [Chat](/earmark-basics/chat). The setting is on by default, and meetings you've recorded always stay visible.

### Finding the join link

Earmark looks for the call link in your event's conferencing details, location, and description. Passcodes stay attached even when the link sits in a formatted description, and an unrelated link, like one to your agenda, doesn't stop Earmark from finding the call. Zoom webinar links work too.

If Earmark can't find a call link, open the event in your calendar and check that it has one.

### Joining your call

When a scheduled meeting is underway, open it in the desktop app. If the event has a call link, a button such as **Join Zoom**, **Join Google Meet**, or **Join Teams** appears above the transcript. Click it to open the call.

This button doesn't start recording, so you can get settled first. Start capture from the meeting's capture controls when you're ready. To join and record in one click, use **Join & capture** from a desktop notification, or click the meeting under **Coming up** in the [Earmark toolbar](/using-earmark/toolbar#coming-up).

The button hides when Earmark detects an active call. To hide it yourself, click **Dismiss meeting link**. It stays hidden for your current visit and can reappear when you reopen the meeting, as long as the scheduled meeting is still happening.

With desktop 1.9.0 or later, supported Zoom links open straight in Zoom instead of going through your browser first. If Zoom isn't installed or the link can't open directly, Earmark falls back to the original web link. Other meeting tools open the way they always have.

## Desktop notifications

Turn on **One click join and capture** in calendar settings to get a notification before each meeting. Click **Join & capture** in the notification to open the call and start recording together. That's the difference from the call button above the transcript, which only opens the call.

To hear a sound when the reminder appears, choose one under **Reminder sound**. You'll hear a preview as you pick. The default is **None**. Click **Show example** to see the full notification.

## Menu bar countdown (macOS)

On macOS, Earmark displays your next meeting and a live countdown directly in the system menu bar. It's on by default. Turn it off with **Show countdown** under **Menu bar** in **Settings > Calendar**.

## Switching providers

To switch from Google to Outlook (or vice versa), disconnect your current calendar first, then connect the new one. Earmark will prompt you to clear any stale upcoming meetings from the previous provider to keep the sidebar clean.

## If your upcoming list looks stale

If a calendar refresh fails, Earmark keeps the last successful list and retries on its next regular refresh. Your existing meetings stay visible, but new changes may take longer to appear.

If a selected calendar is no longer available, Earmark can recover by keeping your other selections or switching to an available default calendar. It only changes the selection after confirming the calendar is gone and a replacement is available. If your list stays stale, review your choices in **Settings > Calendar**.
