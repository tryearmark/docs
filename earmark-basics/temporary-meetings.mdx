## What it does

Temporary meetings let you capture and work with a conversation in real time without saving it to your account or Earmark's servers. Live transcription, tasks, and artifacts work while the meeting is open. When you close it, the transcript, tasks, and artifacts are gone.

This is a mode you turn on per meeting, or require across your whole workspace.

## Why teams use it

- **Sensitive conversations**: personnel discussions, early-stage strategy, or topics where a lasting record isn't appropriate
- **Brainstorming and exploration**: free-flowing sessions where you want live help but don't need history
- **Quick syncs**: short check-ins that don't warrant a place in your meeting history
- **Compliance requirements**: teams that can't keep meeting content on a third-party service

## Start a temporary meeting

<Steps>
<Step title="Open a new meeting">
Click **New meeting**, or use **Quick Capture** from the [toolbar](/using-earmark/toolbar).
</Step>
<Step title="Turn on temporary mode">
Use the temporary meeting toggle in the meeting header before you start capture. The header shows **Temporary meeting** while it's on. Once capture starts, the mode is locked for that meeting.
</Step>
<Step title="Work as usual, then save what you need">
Generate artifacts and copy anything you want to keep before you close the meeting. Earmark warns you before you leave a temporary meeting, because everything in it will be lost.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What's different in a temporary meeting

| Feature | Temporary meeting |
| --- | --- |
| Live transcription, tasks, and artifacts | Work normally while the meeting is open |
| Meeting history, search, and Chat | Not saved, so it won't appear later |
| [Pins](/earmark-basics/pins) | Not available |
| [Projects](/earmark-basics/projects) | Can't be added |
| Chat inside the meeting | Not available |
| Restore after closing | Not possible. Nothing is kept to restore. |

<Tip>
On desktop, [local transcript files](/earmark-basics/local-transcripts) can still save a copy of a temporary meeting to your own device, if you've turned that on. Nothing is stored on Earmark's servers.
</Tip>

## Require temporary mode for a workspace

Workspace admins can make every new meeting temporary. Turn on **Require temporary mode** under **Temporary meetings** in **Settings > Workspace**. When it's on:

- Every new meeting in the workspace is temporary
- Members can't switch it off for individual meetings
- Meetings saved before you turned it on aren't affected

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="shield" title="When this is a good choice">
    Conversations that benefit from live help but shouldn't become part of your team's history: early-stage explorations, sensitive HR discussions, one-off brainstorms, or any session where everyone agrees upfront that nothing should be kept.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="triangle-exclamation" title="What to watch for">
    If action items or decisions need to be revisited later, copy the artifact somewhere safe before you close the meeting. Once it's closed, there's no way to get it back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
