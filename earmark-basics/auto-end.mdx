## What it does

Forgot to hit stop? The desktop app ends a recording on its own once the meeting is over, so it doesn't keep capturing the next meeting or the hallway chat after it.

Auto-end notices when you're done in your meeting app, the moment Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or another meeting app stops using your microphone. There's nothing to set up: it runs automatically on every desktop recording.

<Info>
**Nothing extra is ever recorded.** Auto-end only checks *whether* a meeting app is using your microphone. It never records, listens to, or saves audio from your meeting apps. It sees activity, not content.
</Info>

## When a recording ends

A desktop recording stops when any of these happens:

| What happens | When the recording stops |
| --- | --- |
| **The meeting wraps up** | Earmark follows your calendar to know when a meeting should end, and pays attention to the conversation too. If the meeting runs long, it keeps recording until you're actually done. |
| **No one has spoken for 15 minutes** | Useful when a call ends but your meeting app stays open. |
| **Your computer goes to sleep** | Closing the lid ends the recording cleanly instead of leaving it hanging. |
| **The recording reaches 3 hours** | See [Recording limits](#recording-limits). |

Recordings that aren't tied to a calendar event, like [Quick Capture](/using-earmark/toolbar#quick-capture), end once the conversation is over.

When a recording ends, Earmark saves the transcript and finishes your artifacts, just as if you had clicked **Stop capture** yourself.

## Supported meeting apps

Auto-end recognizes calls in:

- Zoom
- Google Meet in Chrome
- Microsoft Teams
- Slack huddles
- Discord
- FaceTime
- Webex, RingCentral, and GoTo Meeting

## Requirements

- The Earmark desktop app, version **1.8.0** or later, on Mac or Windows
- On a Mac, macOS **14.2** or later

The web app doesn't end recordings automatically. Click **Stop capture** when your meeting is done.

## Recording limits

A single capture session runs for up to **3 hours**, on desktop and web.

1. At 2.5 hours, Earmark shows a **Meeting time approaching limit** notice, so the end never catches you by surprise.
2. At 3 hours, capture stops and Earmark finishes your artifacts.

To keep going, click **Start capture** in the same meeting. The transcript continues in that meeting, and the new session gets another 3 hours. You can do this as many times as you need for workshops and all-day sessions.

## If a recording ends too early

Click **Start capture** in the meeting to pick up where you left off. Everything captured so far stays in the meeting.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn auto-end off?">
    Not currently. If a recording ends before you're done, click **Start capture** in the same meeting to continue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does auto-end record anything extra?">
    No. To tell when a call is over, Earmark checks whether a meeting app is using your microphone. That check never records audio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My meeting app isn't listed. Will auto-end still work?">
    Recordings still stop after 15 minutes without speech, when your computer goes to sleep, and at the 3-hour limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the 3-hour limit?">
    No. The limit is fixed. Start capture again in the same meeting to continue past it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
